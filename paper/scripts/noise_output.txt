====================================================================================================
WHY PER-VERTEX IID JITTER DOES NOT DEFINE A CONTINUOUS OBJECT
====================================================================================================
  The SAME sigma is applied to the SAME object discretised at two vertex
  densities. If the noise were a perturbation of the object, the perturbed
  length would converge; with iid noise it diverges as 1/sqrt(spacing).

h (cm)       vertices      L0 (cm)  iid s=0.5px        ratio correl l=1cm
0.1000           8675     686.0381    1482.5038       2.1610     692.9436
0.0500          17353     686.5264    2619.1294       3.8150     690.6802
0.0250          34713     686.7651    4989.4333       7.2651     690.8584

  The iid column grows without bound under refinement: it is not an object,
  it is a property of the discretisation. That is why the jitter in this
  study is parametrised by (sigma, correlation length) and not by sigma alone.
